# MediRun Company Fact Sheet

## Company Type

MediRun is a small to medium medical delivery company operating around Paris and the inner suburbs.

## Sector

Healthcare logistics and last-mile medical delivery.

## Size

- 5 active drivers.
- 6 vehicles.
- Around 45 to 50 deliveries on a normal day.
- Around 55 to 60 deliveries on a busy Monday.
- Around 35 regular delivery locations.

## Customers

- 1 main hospital.
- Clinics and medical centers.
- Pharmacies in Paris.
- Pharmacies in the inner suburbs.

## Partners

- Pharmacies that receive regular restocking deliveries.
- Clinics that receive medical documents and supplies.
- A main hospital with urgent morning deadlines.
- Suppliers or warehouses preparing medical parcels.

## Competitors

- General courier companies.
- Specialist healthcare logistics companies.
- Pharmacy network internal delivery services.
- On-demand delivery platforms, when they are able to handle medical constraints.

## Context Analysis

The manager currently has to organize many deliveries manually. This becomes stressful on busy mornings, especially when urgent hospital deliveries, refrigerated products, driver hours, and vehicle capacity all have to be considered at the same time.

The digital solution should support planning and traceability. It does not need to be a real GPS tracking system for the first version. The priority is to help the manager generate a clear daily plan and help each driver see their assigned route.
